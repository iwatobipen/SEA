Contributing ligand pairs

Calculation details & provenance

Raw score sums qualifying pair similarities. Statistical evaluation uses a calibrated Gumbel model or Monte Carlo sampling. With Monte Carlo, equal E-values can occur at the sampling resolution; raw score breaks ranking ties. Z-scores are unavailable when the fitted variance is zero.
